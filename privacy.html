Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy describes the personal-use assistant operated by Jorgen Molen (the “Application”). It is not a public service and is intended for use by its owner only.

Google account access

With the owner's authorization, the Application may access Gmail information needed to perform requested email tasks, including finding, reading, organizing, and sending messages, depending on the permissions granted.

How information is used

Google account information is used to carry out the owner's requested assistant functions. The Application is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale of personal information.

Storage and security

Authentication tokens and any locally processed data are managed in the owner's private computing environment. Access to that environment is controlled by the owner. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Disclosure and service providers

The Application is not offered to third-party users. Data may be processed by connected service providers when necessary to carry out an owner-requested function, subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. Google user data is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the Application's local environment and can remove stored credentials and local data. Access to Google services can also be revoked through the owner's Google Account security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the Application's operator through the support contact listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
